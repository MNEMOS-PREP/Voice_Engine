Which interviewer sounds like a person?

The same lines in each candidate voice. "Today" is the voice students hear now, played live by your browser: open this page in Microsoft Edge for it.

Names shown

The cloned voices are DailyTalk's two speakers (Lee et al., ICASSP 2023; CC BY-SA 4.0), recorded for conversational speech-synthesis research, cloned here from about 12 seconds of their own conversation. Research only: a dataset licence is not a speaker's consent to become a product voice. Chatterbox marks every clip with an inaudible watermark.

"Written" lines are what the interviewer writes today; "spoken" lines say the same thing the way people talk. Comparing the two shows how much is the voice and how much is the wording. Under each clip: how long this laptop's CPU took to make it. Every clip plays at normal speed; a voice that takes longer to make than to play cannot answer live without a faster computer.
